# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, collaborators, open-source maintainers, and technically curious peers evaluating Srinivas's work. They need to understand his capabilities, see concrete evidence, and reach his profiles quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Srinivas Sivaratri as a self-directed engineer who builds practical AI systems, developer tools, and open-source contributions. Success means a visitor can grasp the focus of his work, inspect relevant projects, and make contact without friction.

## Brand Personality

Direct, inventive, and technically assured. The experience should feel focused and distinctive without becoming theatrical or difficult to use.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, ornamental terminal cosplay, dense résumé walls, soft card grids, muted low-contrast portfolios, and motion that competes with the work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with evidence: let shipped projects and merged contributions carry the story.
- Reward curiosity without hiding the primary path.
- Keep the interface as precise and legible as the engineering it represents.
- Use a few memorable interactions with restraint.
- Make every project and contact path work equally well with touch, mouse, or keyboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible focus, semantic structure, keyboard access, sufficient contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
