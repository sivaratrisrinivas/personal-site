# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, collaborators, open-source maintainers, recruiters and technically curious peers evaluating Srinivas's work. Some want the facts in thirty seconds; others will happily wander for ten minutes if the site gives them a reason to. Both must be served.

## Product Purpose

Present Srinivas Sivaratri as a self-directed engineer who builds practical AI systems, developer tools and open-source contributions, in a way people remember and want to explore. Success means a visitor can find the positioning line, the roles, the upstream pull requests, the two projects (What / Why / How) and a direct contact path, and that someone who is curious keeps digging until they have found the details underneath.

## Brand Personality

Playful, inventive and honest, with a weird streak. A small colourful world made with care, where the jokes (calibrated signposts, a button that refuses to act, a mail-leaking post office) are all riffs on the actual work: measuring whether a model is right and refusing to act when it is not.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, ornamental terminal cosplay, dense résumé walls, findings walls, capability clusters, and a game that hides the work behind a puzzle with no way out. Also: games with fake-sounding stats or invented chips.

## Design Principles

- The site is the game. There is no plainer site hiding behind it.
- Reward curiosity: details sit near the thing they are about, hints are free (the Index), and finding them all ends in a conversation, not a lock.
- Never make anyone play to read: the Index lists everything as text, and the Résumé and Say hi buttons are always in the top bar.
- Be weird on purpose, and honest: every number and claim comes from the real work, and anything that is only a joke reads as one. Never invent a chip.
- One source of content: every word of the real material lives once, in the page's codex, and is reused by the game.
- Make every interaction work with keyboard, mouse and touch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible focus, keyboard access, a text alternative to the whole game (Index and the no-JavaScript codex), sufficient contrast, 44px touch targets and reduced-motion support. Day and night follow the system until the visitor chooses; that choice persists locally.
