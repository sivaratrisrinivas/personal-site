# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, collaborators, open-source maintainers, and technically curious peers evaluating Srinivas's work. They need a short profile, experience, upstream contributions, eight shipped projects, and a direct contact path.

## Product Purpose

Present Srinivas Sivaratri as a self-directed engineer who builds practical AI systems, developer tools, and open-source contributions. Success means a visitor can read the positioning line, scan experience and upstream writeups, read one project list (What / Why / How), and make contact without friction.

## Brand Personality

Direct, inventive, and technically assured, with a weird streak. The way in is a game (a hedge maze with a strict judge) that rewards curiosity, and it never gets in the way of someone who just wants the facts.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, ornamental terminal cosplay, dense résumé walls, duplicate featured-plus-archive lists, findings walls, capability clusters, and a game that hides the work behind a puzzle with no way out.

## Design Principles

- Lead with evidence: let shipped projects, measured one-liners, and honest Live / Measured / CI chips carry the story. Never invent a chip.
- Two doors, one source of content: the maze and the plain page show the same words, and the plain page is always one click away.
- Play on the way in, plain on demand: no one is made to play. Recruiters get the résumé and email from the first screen.
- Keep the interface as precise and legible as the engineering it represents.
- Be weird on purpose, and honest: anything standing in for real work (the model replay) says so on screen. Never invent a chip.
- Make every project and contact path work equally well with touch, mouse, or keyboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible focus, semantic structure, keyboard access, sufficient contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Appearance follows the system theme until the visitor chooses otherwise; that choice persists locally and keeps focus rings and `theme-color` in sync.
